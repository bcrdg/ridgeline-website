Questions Sonora-Area Businesses Ask Us

Do you provide onsite IT support in Sonora?

Yes. Our helpdesk is based here, so most issues are fixed remotely within minutes, and a technician can be at your office the same day when something needs hands on site.

How do you keep us running during PG&E power shutoffs and outages?

Foothill businesses lose power and internet more often than offices in the valley. We plan for it with battery backup on critical equipment, backups stored offsite in the cloud, and backup internet such as Starlink or cellular failover, so a shutoff doesn't mean lost data or a dead office when the power comes back.

Our internet out here isn't great. Can you still support us?

Yes. Most of our support is remote and works on slower rural connections, and we can help you choose and set up a better or backup connection for your location.

What should I do if our business email gets hacked?

Call us right away at (209) 213-2088. Don't keep using the account, and don't pay any invoice or wire request that came from it. We lock the account down, find out what the attacker accessed or forwarded, remove any hidden mail rules, and check whether customers or vendors were sent fraudulent messages.

Do you work with local government and public agencies?

Yes. We support county departments, special districts and other public organizations, including their security and documentation requirements.

Can you help with HIPAA, NIST or other compliance requirements?

Yes. We perform HIPAA security risk assessments for dental and healthcare offices and align environments to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. We fix what the assessment finds and give you the documentation to show an auditor, insurer or client.

How much does managed IT cost?

It depends on how many people and devices you have and what compliance you need. We quote a flat monthly price after a free Pulse Check, so you know the number before you commit.

What happens after hours?

Your systems are watched around the clock by our 24/7/365 Security Operations Center, which escalates directly to our on-call technician. For businesses that need it, we also offer 24/7/365 desktop support from our own local technicians, never an outsourced call center.
